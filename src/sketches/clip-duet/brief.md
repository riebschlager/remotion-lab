# Clip Duet

## Idea
A psychedelic poster: a rainbow bust stays calm in the centre while a saturated
collage tunnel breathes behind it. Two independent musical phrases, no typography.

## Rhythm
120 BPM, four bars of 4/4. The centre GIF fits once per bar. The video retriggers
on a swung eighth-note pattern [1, 0, .45, 0, .75, 0, 0, .35]. Rests keep playback
moving; fades reach zero at every restart. Accent strength controls its brightness.

## Look
Black canvas, tunnel filling it but dim enough to leave the bust readable. The
bust sits in a square 72 vmin wide, centred, with a fine violet frame. Portrait
and landscape preserve the same focal hierarchy.

## Assets
- test/Art Animation GIF by Mr Tronch.gif: opaque square 480×480, 30 frames,
  seamless colour cycle, held frames. Fit to one bar, contain.
- test/slapchop-video-20260824-205001-10s.mp4: 1080×1920, 300 frames,
  non-looping mirrored collage. Retrigger with a two-beat playback duration,
  hold at the end through long rests; fade between hits hides restart cuts.
Both manifest entries and filmstrips inspected before design. Internal cuts in
this video remain part of its collage look; the fade only hides restart cuts.
