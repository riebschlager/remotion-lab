# Milhouse Breaking News

## Original request

> ok. new sketch idea! this one is anchored around milhouse-02/Falling.fbx here's the gag: it's a "breaking news" type of display, crt effects and all. the breaking news is that a giant milhouse is floating above earth. the falling milhouse is Falling.fbx which is an fbx of a falling motion for the milhouse mesh. it should include a crawl of news update with gags about a huge milhouse floating above earth. it should cut to different scenes in the breaking news video feed. shots from space, shots from cities looking up ad a huge milhouse floating above. radar tracking images. let's have some fun with it.

## Treatment

An overly serious local news bulletin. Giant Milhouse and the curve of Earth reveal the gag, two ground feeds make his size absurd, and the weather desk tracks him like a storm. A warm ivory headline and red breaking-news slug anchor the composition. Tungsten and violet light distinguish the street cameras from the cold orbital feed, and radar gets its own green phosphor palette. Use framed feeds, soft broadcast color bleed, scanlines, a rolling beam and a brief tracking glitch on each cut.

Default to a silent 20-second loop at 120 BPM, 4/4, ten bars. Give each camera two bars with a hard on-beat switch, with the seam in the middle of the satellite wide shot. A second orbital camera gets a tighter, tilted optical track. The animated figure drifts independently from the edits. Radar sweeps and the crawl keep moving at the seam. Portrait is the default, with type and ground geometry recomposed for landscape.

## Asset evidence

The scanner records the new collection but skips FBX. The local source is FBX 7700 with one skinned mesh, an embedded palette and a 4.10-second Mixamo take. Inspected joint and mesh bounds show a spread-limbed fall with little root travel and the head pointing along positive Z. Load the declared collection file directly. Seek its skeletal animation from beat phase, rocking between the endpoints to close the motion without an asset cut. Clip and Type's collection-based playback do not apply to this model and the static broadcast labels. All Earth, radar and city scenery is drawn in the sketch.

## On-air copy

The headline reads “GIANT MILHOUSE FLOATS ABOVE EARTH.” The feeds are “ORBITAL CAM,” “SPRINGFIELD,” “SHELBYVILLE,” “DOPPLER 6,” and “ORBITAL CAM 2.” The updates crawl through four reports.

- FAA REROUTES AROUND LEFT SHOE
- MOON DECLINES TO COMMENT
- TIDES NOW PRESCRIPTION STRENGTH
- LOCAL MOM PACKS ORBITAL LUNCH

## Review criteria

The figure and its glasses should read at phone size. Each switch should immediately suggest a different source, the ground feeds should feel like looking up from street level, and the crawl should stay readable under the CRT treatment. Inspect before and after the loop point and both default canvas orientations.

## Preview review

In portrait the satellite wide shot keeps the glasses clear above Earth's limb. The downtown shot places dark buildings on both sides of the figure. The harbor trades those tall walls for the low rail and moon. The radar changes the image and palette for a full two bars, and the optical camera returns to color in a tighter frame. Landscape puts the radar readings alongside the scope and shows more of the city and Earth around the figure. The seam stays inside the orbital wide shot and the crawl continues across it. Both contact sheets passed the frame-zero repeat and seam checks.
