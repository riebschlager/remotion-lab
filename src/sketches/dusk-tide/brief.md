# Dusk Tide

## Original brief

> Make a sketch called dusk-tide: a quiet, suspended little loop, like breathing
> beside still water after sunset. 84 BPM, four bars of 3/4. Use procedural shapes
> only: no text, photographs, clips or fonts. Indigo, muted peach and a little
> warm light. I want to feel the pulse without everything blinking together.
> Give it one memorable central form and a quieter answering gesture. It should
> work as both a portrait post and a landscape backdrop. Keep it spare.

Supplied: tempo, meter, bars, no media or copy, palette family, one central
form with a quieter answer, both canvases. Assumed: the crescent, the water
arcs, the six-beat breath and the 1.5-beat answer delay below.

## Idea and hierarchy

The central form is a broad, organic crescent: an almost-closed peach vessel of
light, hanging over water. It is the first thing to notice. A small warm seam
along its lower edge gives it weight without a bright spotlight. Three shallow
indigo-peach water arcs below answer its breath, remaining identifiable even at
rest. The empty field stays quiet.

## Rhythm

84 BPM, four bars of 3/4, twelve beats. The crescent expands once every two
bars; a full breath takes six beats. A restrained, single-cycle seeded drift
makes it feel suspended. The response breath is delayed by 1.5 beats, strongest
around beat 4.5 while the central form has begun to settle. The backdrop has no
beat flash. All trajectories and envelopes are smooth and periodic.

## Look and copy

Indigo field, muted peach crescent, a little warm light along its lower edge.
No copy. Must not appear: text, photographs, clips, fonts, beat flashes.

## Assets and treatment

None. Procedural SVG only; no cuts.

## Canvas and success criteria

Portrait first: crescent near the upper middle, water answer below, ample dark
margins. Landscape: same vmin-scaled focal group, centered with generous
horizontal negative space. Neither shape nor glow may crop.

The crescent stays readable throughout. The answer arcs visibly widen and lift
after the main breath and are recognizable at phone size without competing with
the crescent. Exposed controls: palette, form size, breath length in bars,
breath depth, response delay and response strength. Breath cycles must fit the
loop, and the response delay must be shorter than the breath phrase.

## Review notes

Inspected both contact sheets, then stills at frames 65 (beat 3.04, before the
answer), 96 (beat 4.48, its peak) and 129 (beat 6.02, after), since the default
sheet does not sample the answer's peak. The first thin water arcs were too
faint at phone size; stroke went from 0.19 to 0.32 vmin and base opacity from
0.24 to 0.36, so the response reads while staying secondary. The crescent stays
dominant and nothing crops on either canvas. The seam reads as ordinary smooth
motion, with no intentional hit or cut.

Visual timing rounds the loop to 257 frames at 30 fps (8.567 s), about 84.047
effective BPM.
